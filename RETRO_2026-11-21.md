## 3. Root causes

### 3.1 RC1: "VOID" marks an order cancelled without checking that it was (AMLP, CPER, SPY)
`sync_closures()` ([ib_exec.py:1322](dashboard/execution/ib_exec.py:1322)): when the journal
resolves a trade before the broker entry fills, the code calls `ib.cancelOrder(o.order)` and
**immediately** writes `ib_mirror.status='VOID'`. It never checks that the cancel worked. From
then on VOID is terminal: the row is skipped forever and no code looks at those orders again.

The cancel can fail silently. `cancelOrder()` only works from the clientId that placed the
order (Error 10147, documented in HANDOFF 2026-09-01). The July orders came from an earlier
client/session, before the Oct 14 move to clientIds 41–44. The resting GTC SELL-STP legs
survived and filled months later, once price reached the old stop levels. Each one *opened* a
short, because there was no long position for it to close. The Sep-era logs have rotated out,
so the 10147 detail is the most likely mechanism rather than one seen directly. The fills
themselves are confirmed.

### 3.2 RC2 + RC3: QQQ, an oversized stop plus a blind re-protect
- **RC2 (bracket not resized).** On Oct 14 the mirror-qty heal corrected #79 from 8 to 7
  shares to match the broker. **It did not resize the resting stop**, which was still for 8.
  On Oct 30 the stop sold 8 against 7 held, leaving the account at **−1**.
- **RC3 (reprotect ignores what the broker holds).** About 4 seconds later,
  `reprotect_naked_positions()` ([ib_exec.py:1955](dashboard/execution/ib_exec.py:1955)) found
  no resting order on QQQ (the OCA group had just been used up). It took the **journal's**
  direction (long) and the **mirror's** qty (7), and re-armed a SELL bracket. It never read the
  broker's signed position, which was −1 and logged one line earlier as "broker position is
  short". The new stop sat right at the market and filled immediately, leaving the account at **−8**.
  This is the third bug in this one function (2026-08-18 and 2026-09-01 were the first two).

### 3.3 RC4: the exit could not cover, then stopped trying
- `manual_close_position()` sent `BUY 7 MKT` (`exit#79`). IBKR rejected it with **"Order was
  discarded"** more than 100 times between Oct 30 and Nov 12. Likely causes: the order reused
  `held.contract` from `ib.positions()` (`exch=NASDAQ`, a direct route, not SMART), and it sent
  MKT orders outside regular trading hours too (01:02 UTC attempts).
- Even if it had filled, it would have bought 7, not 8. The quantity was capped at the
  *trade's* size, not the broker's position.
- On Nov 12, #79's mirror row became CLOSED, so the retries stopped and QQQ became just
  another "untracked" symbol.
