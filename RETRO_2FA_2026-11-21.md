# Retro — 2026-11-21: the LIVE gateway costs a YubiKey touch almost every day

**Trigger.** The user is asked for a passkey (YubiKey) login roughly every day. The goal set on
2026-10-17 (MIN-2FA) was **one planned touch per week**. Today, Sat Nov 21, the gateway is down
again after a reboot, waiting for yet another touch.

**Verdict.** In the 31 days from Oct 22 to Nov 21 the live gateway completed **26 full passkey
logins**. Only **4** of them were the weekly re-authentication IBKR requires. The other 22 came
from six separate causes. Three of those causes are entirely ours (a redundant cold restart, a
second live gateway in Docker, the box going down), one is IBKR-side but not yet understood
(token rejected at the daily restart), and the MIN-2FA design either assumed them away or never
shipped the fix (§4).

Evidence sources (all on this machine, nothing inferred from memory):

- `C:\IBC-Live\Logs\archived\*\{IBC-*.txt,launcher.log}`: 55 snapshots of the IBC and gateway logs taken before each launch

## 3. Root causes

### 3.1 The weekly cold restart adds a second weekly touch, and it drifts with Windows' timezone (ours)

`C:\IBC-Live\config.ini:643 ColdRestartTime=20:30`.
- IBKR already forces the weekly full auth on Sunday: `Re-login is required` on Oct 18 18:38,
  Nov 1 16:54, Nov 8 17:18 HKT. The daily restart after that point becomes a full shutdown
  (Nov 8 20:26 `Shutdown progress`). A cold restart on top of that only **adds** a touch
  (Nov 1: 18:49 and 20:37).
- IBC computes the cold-restart moment in **Windows local time** (the `Starting Gateway` line
  is PST, and the cold restart fired at "20:30" = 12:30 HKT). The gateway's own daily
  `AutoRestartTime` runs in the gateway's **HKT** clock. Once Windows moved to PST, the weekly
  cold restart landed on **Monday 12:30 HKT** (Nov 9, Nov 16), a full day after IBKR's re-auth.
  That made 2 touches every week.
- MIN-2FA P5 kept the cold restart deliberately ("an unattended mid-week expiry would be
  unpredictable"). The evidence says IBKR's weekly expiry happens on Sunday regardless, so
  the cold restart buys nothing.

### 3.2 IBKR rejects the daily token about 30% of the time (IBKR-side, not understood)

Of 17 observable warm restarts (Oct 17 → Nov 20), **10 were accepted** (`NS_AUTH_START
initialTokenType=TST` → `Login has completed`). **6 were rejected** with
`Authorization failed: The security tokens associated with your login credentials have expired
(routinely) in accordance with our security protocols`. One more failed with `Server
disconnected, please try again`.
- After a rejection the gateway shows a `Gateway` message box and sits `LOGGED_OUT`. A full
  passkey login is then unavoidable. The 2-minute watchdog grace is therefore **not** the
  problem, and a longer grace would not have saved these.
- Session age does not explain it: rejections came 1.2 h, 9 h, 22–24 h after the full login,
  and acceptances came 0.5 h, 9 h, 21 h and several chained days after.
- Hypotheses, to be tested by the spec (§6 of `IBKR_WEEKLY_2FA_SPEC.md`):
  - **(H1)** Competing logins for the same username invalidate the token: the Docker gateway's
    daily attempts (§3.3), `Existing session detected` Nov 3 00:20, or the user's own IBKR
    mobile/web logins. Consistent with 4 of 6 rejections. **Inconsistent** with 3 acceptances
    that also followed a Docker attempt. Not proven.
  - **(H2)** IBKR-side routine rotation that we cannot influence.
  - **(H3, confirmed for 2 of 7; added 2026-11-21 evening)** The daily router reboot
    (`D:\claude\xiaomi-router-reboot.vbs`, internet offline ~40–100 s) ran at **05:30:01 HKT**
    every day until Nov 4, the same minute as the gateway's then-05:30 warm restart. Oct 23
    (token rejected 05:31:21, router offline 05:30:01–05:31:02) and Oct 27 (`Server disconnected`
    05:30:27, router offline 05:30:01–05:30:56) are this collision. Oct 24/25 warm restarts at
    05:32 came after the router was back and were accepted. The other **5 rejections**
    (Nov 2, 3, 5, 6, 20) were at the 20:30 HKT warm restart, nowhere near a router reboot.
    The router reboot and the Docker daily restart **on their own** never cost a touch:
    none of the 26 touches follows one, and the gateway reconnects after the outage
    without re-authenticating.
- The `Server disconnected, please try again` failure (Oct 27) is transient. Neither IBC nor
  our scripts retry the token, so one network blip cost a touch.
- **Router reboot moved into US trading hours (fixed 2026-11-21 19:15 HKT).** The launcher
  skipped the legacy 04:30 HKT fire by checking "Windows local hour < 5". Its two task
  triggers are UTC-anchored (20:30Z / 21:30Z), so from Nov 4 (PST, then EST) the 04:30 HKT
  fire read as 12:30/15:30 local, rebooted the router, and the 05:30 fire was eaten by the
  60-min cooldown. Result: a ~1-minute internet outage at **15:30 New York, every trading
  day Nov 5–21**, inside the last half hour of RTH. Fix: the guard now computes HKT from UTC
  (WMI, registry fallback) and both router logs are stamped `... HKT`; dry-run verified at
  04:30/04:59 → skip, 05:00/05:30 → reboot, with Windows on EST. Backups `*.bak-20261121`.

### 3.3 A second LIVE gateway in Docker logs into the same account every day (ours)

`quant-ibgateway-live-docker` was "parked" on 2026-10-13 behind the compose profile
`docker-gateway`, and its hold file was deleted by hand on Oct 14. Compose profiles only affect
`docker compose up`. The **container already existed with `restart=unless-stopped`**, so the
Docker daemon starts it on every WSL boot. `Docker Daily Restart` (`wsl --shutdown` at 20:45
HKT) boots WSL every day.
- Result: **56 LIVE login attempts since Oct 14** (44 since Oct 22). There is one every day at
  12:45Z (20:45 HKT) and one after every Windows boot. Each one enters the password and opens a
  passkey challenge that can never complete (Linux JxBrowser has no PIN UI, per
  `IBKR_GATEWAY_PASSKEY_SPEC.md`).
- Collisions: every post-boot Windows prompt that died within 25–50 s had a Docker challenge
  opened seconds before it: Oct 19, Nov 1, Nov 6, Nov 12, Nov 19, Nov 21. Those are wasted
  prompts and re-prompts, and on Nov 19 they added up to IBKR's `Too many failed login attempts`
  throttle (Docker 13:23Z + 7 Windows launches).
- It may also be H1 in §3.2.
- The MIN-2FA statement "the Docker daily restart never touches the gateway/token (separate
  OS domain)" was true for the Windows process and **false for the IBKR account**.

### 3.4 The box goes down several times a week (ours: hardware and habit)

Oct 22 → Nov 21:
- **5 unexpected power losses** (Kernel-Power 41: Nov 1, 4, 6, 12, 13)
- **7 Start-menu restarts** (Nov 1, Nov 4, Nov 12, Nov 19 ×3, Nov 21)
- **1 Windows Update restart** clicked from the notification (Nov 15; TrustedInstaller then
  rebooted a second time)
- **1 shutdown with fast startup** (Oct 22)
- **3 sleeps** (Nov 6 for 7.5 h; Nov 12 and Nov 17 were short, and the session survived)

A session cannot survive any of these: the `autorestart` token is only written at a healthy
scheduled restart. `NoAutoRebootWithLoggedOnUsers=1` (MIN-2FA P2) only stops *automatic* WU
reboots. A user clicking "Restart now" bypasses it.

### 3.5 Our recovery chain lengthens outages (ours)

- **Abandoned-session modal.** When `ibkr-live-login.ps1` finds IBC alive with no passkey
  prompt, it asks with a blocking `OKCancel`. This happened 15 times. It sat unanswered for
  14 h (Oct 23), 13.6 h (Oct 27), 10.5 h (Nov 6), 2.5 h (Nov 20) and 55 min (Nov 6). In every
  token-rejection case the right action was simply "start a fresh login". The modal turned a
  1-touch event into hours of darkness. GATEWAY_AUTOLOGIN §4 (silent completion) specified the
  fix on Oct 18. **It was never implemented**: every `scripts/windows/*.ps1` is dated
  Oct 17–18.
- **Terminal `Say()` MessageBox.** The script keeps running after it gives up, and the watchdog
  treats a live script as "login in progress" and stops re-prompting. This is today's state.
- **Archives taken at the wrong moment.** Archives are taken when the script starts, not right
  before IBC launches. Logs of sessions killed after a long modal wait are lost, e.g. the
  Nov 20 04:30→07:03 PST session. The logon autostart never archives at all, which is why the
  Nov 15 pre-reboot session (did IBKR send `Re-login is required` that Sunday?) is lost.

### 3.6 Nobody was told reliably (ours)

- **Telegram cap.** `gateway:*` pages shared the global 10/day cap with every other
  `gateway-push.sh` caller. Today the gateway-down page was `suppressed-cap` behind an
  **unrelated infra flood**: `task-heartbeat-check.ps1`, using the legacy single-argument call
  (key `legacy`), sent a critical every 10 min about RustDesk/router task heartbeats (§2 item 5).
  Two defects: the gateway page had no reserved budget, and a legacy caller with no key or
  level is treated as `critical`. On Nov 19, two pages `failed` because they were sent during
  the reboots.
- **Dead self-audit.** `\IBKR\Restart Observe` / `\IBKR\Restart Verify` still fire every day at
  20:29/20:34/20:50 HKT, but they end with `LastTaskResult=0x1` / `0x41306`.
  `restart-observe.log` was last written **Oct 17** and `restart-verify.log` **Oct 18**. The daily
  PASS/FAIL verdict MIN-2FA promised ("two clean evenings = claim proven") has not run for
  34 days, and nothing noticed.

### 3.7 Gateway restart time flips between 05:30 and 20:30 (ours/unclear, minor)

`config.ini` still says `AutoRestartTime=08:30 PM`. The 05:30 migration (GATEWAY_AUTOLOGIN
Phase 1) was **never done**. Yet the gateway restarted at **05:30** on Oct 23–28, and IBC logged
`Auto restart time changed from 05:30 AM to 08:30 PM` on several logins (once `from 08:30 AM`).
So a stored 05:30 AM value keeps resurfacing from the gateway's own settings, and IBC's
correction does not always survive the next restart. This is harmless for touch count, but the
restart moment is not under our control.

## 4. Why MIN-2FA "1 planned touch/week" isn't holding

| MIN-2FA assumption (Oct 17–18) | Reality (Oct 22 → Nov 21) |
|---|---|
| The daily warm restart reuses the token every day | IBKR rejected it 6 of 17 times (+1 transient) |
| The Sunday cold restart is *the* single weekly touch | IBKR forces its own Sunday re-auth first. The cold restart is a second touch, and since the TZ change it lands on Monday |
| The Docker daily restart never touches the gateway | It restarts a **second live gateway** that logs into the same account daily |
| The WU policy prevents surprise reboots | 14 reboots, shutdowns and power losses + 3 sleeps. The WU reboot came via a user click |
| The box's clock is HKT | 4 timezone changes (PST for 17 days, EST now). IBC's cold-restart math follows the Windows clock |
| Watchdog grace (2 min) avoids false alarms; auto-login fixes the rest | Grace is fine. The **login script's blocking modals** made outages hours long |
| 20:50 verify pushes a daily verdict | Tasks failing silently since Oct 17 |
| Pages arrive | Gateway pages shared a cap with an unrelated infra flood (`task-heartbeat-check`, legacy key) |
| GATEWAY_AUTOLOGIN Phases 1–3 (05:30, silent completion, staged token, page ladder) | Spec committed Oct 18. **None implemented** |

## 5. IBKR hard limits vs our bugs

| IBKR forces (cannot be engineered away) | Ours (fixable) |
|---|---|
| Passkey with user verification on every full auth → a human touch (SFC HK mandate) | Redundant `ColdRestartTime`, and its dependence on the Windows clock |
| A weekly full re-auth around the Sunday reset (`Re-login is required`) | The Docker live gateway still running, with `restart=unless-stopped` |
| One active session per username. A second login competes | Reboots, sleep, power losses (UPS, power plan, habit) |
| The gateway must restart or log off daily. The token survives only a *healthy* scheduled restart | No retry on a transient `Server disconnected` |
| `Too many failed login attempts` throttle | Blocking modals in the unattended login path |
| **Unknown:** the "security tokens have expired (routinely)" rejections (§3.2). They may be IBKR policy, or may be caused by competing logins. **Must be measured after the Docker gateway is gone** | Gateway pages subject to the global Telegram cap; dead self-audit |

## 6. What is still wrong / unknown

1. **The token-rejection cause (§3.2) is not identified.** If it is IBKR policy, then
   "1 touch/week" is **not achievable** with daily restarts. The realistic floor would be about
   1 + 1.6/week. The spec gates the target on a two-week measurement.
2. **Who ran `tzutil`** (Nov 4 ×4, Nov 21 ×1), and is the user now in a US timezone? That
   decides where the weekly touch should land.
3. **What causes the five power losses.** No crash dump or bugcheck was looked at; only
   Kernel-Power 41 was seen.
4. **Whether IBKR mobile/web logins by the user expire the gateway token.** This is H1, part c,
   and is testable.

## The one-line lesson

> We designed for the one restart we scheduled and stopped looking. **Every other way the
> session dies (a second gateway, a reboot, a timezone, IBKR rejecting its own token) was
> outside the model, and the audit that would have shown it died the day after it shipped.**
> Count touches from the logs every week, not from the design.
